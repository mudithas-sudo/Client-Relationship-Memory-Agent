# Incident INC-0842 Service Credit Memo (FICTIONAL TRAINING DATA)

Client: LankaPay (Pvt) Ltd. Date of incident: 2026-09-14, 11:22-13:05 IST.

## Summary
Degraded switching (Severity 1) for 1 h 43 min caused by a failed HSM
failover during a routine certificate rotation. 38,412 authorisations were
queued and retried; 1,204 timed out permanently.

## Availability impact
September availability landed at 99.76%, which per SLA Addendum v2 s.4 puts
the service credit at 10% of the monthly recurring fee (USD 980).

## Actions agreed
1. Credit of USD 980 applied to the October invoice (claimed 2026-09-18,
   within the 60-day window).
2. HSM failover runbook amended; dual-operator rule for certificate rotation.
3. Client requested a quarterly failover drill - commercial impact TBD; to be
   raised in the November account review.
