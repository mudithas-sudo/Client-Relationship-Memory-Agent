# Ceylon Microfinance - Core Banking Lite Quote (FICTIONAL TRAINING DATA)

Prepared for: Ceylon Microfinance Ltd (fictional). Prepared by: Presales Team.

## 1. Scope
Core Banking Lite for 8 branches and 2 mobile units; savings, micro-loans,
and field-officer tablet app. Single-phase rollout over 14 weeks.

## 2. Commercials
Licence: USD 42,000 per year. Implementation fee: USD 18,500 one-time.
Support: 15% of licence fee, business hours. A 4% discount applies if the
annual licence is prepaid before contract signature.

## 3. Technical
Hosting: vendor cloud (shared tenancy), data residency in Sri Lanka.
Integration: LankaPay CEFTS for disbursements; SMS OTP via cPaaS gateway.
