# Golden Lotus Hotels - Integration Scope Note (FICTIONAL TRAINING DATA)

Prepared after the 2026-09-22 technical workshop at Colombo head office.

## Systems
- Opera PMS v5.6 (on-premise at each property; nightly sync to head office)
- Service Desk: Freshservice (cloud)
- Loyalty: in-house "Lotus Club" MySQL 8 database

## Decisions from the workshop
1. Booking confirmations trigger from the head-office Opera sync, NOT per
   property, to avoid 11 separate connectors. Accepted latency: up to 15 min.
2. Check-in links go out at 07:00 hotel-local time on arrival day.
3. In-stay requests create Freshservice tickets via webhook; SLA 10 min
   first response, tracked on the client side.
4. Lotus Club tier (Silver/Gold/Jade) must appear in message personalisation;
   read-only access to a nightly CSV export was agreed - no direct DB access.

## Open items
- WhatsApp template approvals for 3 brands (client marketing owns copy).
- Data residency: guest phone numbers must remain in Sri Lanka; confirm the
  vendor's WhatsApp BSP routing satisfies this before contract.
